# Healthcare-Analysis-Project

This project is based on the Healthcare performance of different hospitals located in different parts of United States. 

Tools used: 

- Power BI

- DAX

Key Insights:

i) Total no. of patients -> 55500 or 56K (roundoff value).

ii) Total no. of doctors -> 40K

iii) Female patients among total patients -> 28K (50%)

iv) Male patients among total patients -> 22K (40%)

v) Non-binary patients among total patients -> 6K (10%)

vi) Most common blood types among patients is -> A+ (35%) then followed by O+ (25%) and O- (15%)

vii) Senior aged patients are the most common age group ( age of 56 years and above) among the other age groups.

viii) Diabetes, Hypertension and Obesity are the medical conditions which are diagnosed the most in comparison to other medical conditions. Which means patients having these 3 medical
      conditions are greater in number as well.
      
ix) Patients average stay by medical condition is 15.51 days.

x) Average billing amount for treatment is $25.54K and total billing amount for treatment is $1.42bn

xi) Hypertension and Diabetes charge the highest in terms of treatment costs.

xii) Houston Methodist Hospital earns the most revenue -> $520.98M

xiii) Medicare Insurance company covers the most patient treatment costs with a total billing amount of $70,74,70,440.24

xiv) Houston Methodist Hospital treats the most number of patients in terms of patient outcomes as well as treats greater number of patients.

xv) Aspirin, Lipitor, Paracetamol and Penicilin are the medications which are prescribed the most for different medical conditions.

xvi) Most number of patients are admitted in Elective and Urgent admission types (19K) followed by Emergency type (18K)

xvii) Medicare Insurance company is covering the most patients followed by United HealthCare Insurance company for each test result (Abnormal, Inconclusive, Normal).

xviii) Eastern part of the country has the most number of hospitals thereby covering the most number of patients in total (4-5 hospitals in total in the eastern side of the country).
